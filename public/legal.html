Verification Disclaimer & Product Policy

Last updated: 24 May 2026

Summary

  • Harmoniq provides tools to help identify possible copyright matches using fingerprints, metadata, and third-party services.
  • Results are best-effort and do not constitute legal advice or a definitive ownership determination.

Accuracy and Limits

Fingerprint and metadata matching may produce false positives and false negatives. Confidence scores are indicative, not definitive. External providers (AcoustID, Gracenote, Audible Magic, etc.) each have their own matching models and coverage; availability and accuracy vary.

User Responsibilities

  • You are responsible for obtaining any necessary licenses or permissions from rights holders before publishing or monetizing content.
  • Use Harmoniq's request-tracking features to document outreach, but maintain additional records as needed.

No Liability

Harmoniq and its maintainers are not liable for damages arising from reliance on verification results. Use this service at your own risk and consult a legal professional for binding guidance.

Contact

For questions or to report incorrect matches, open an issue in the project repository or contact the listed maintainer in README.md.
