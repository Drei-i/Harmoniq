Harmoniq Pitch Deck Outline

Slide 1: Title
- Harmoniq
- Unified music discovery and copyright verification for content creators
- Presenters: [Your Name] and [Friend's Name]

Slide 2: Company Purpose
- Harmoniq helps creators find safe, copyright-friendly music and verify audio before publishing.
- One platform combining track discovery, licensing clarity, playlist management, and audio fingerprinting.
- Target audience: content creators making videos, podcasts, social media, and online courses.

Slide 3: Problem
- Creators waste hours hopping between multiple libraries and license pages.
- Copyright rules are confusing, especially for non-commercial and remix use.
- No single product currently gives both music discovery and quick verification in the same flow.

Slide 4: Solution
- Harmoniq bundles discovery, licensing interpretation, and verification in one app.
- Search CC/public domain music with beginner-friendly icons and clear usage guidance.
- Verify tracks with audio fingerprinting to reduce copyright risk before publishing.

Slide 5: Why Now
- Rapid creator economy growth means more independent content and more copyright risk.
- Platforms are enforcing rules more aggressively, so creators need safer workflows.
- Creators are ready for tools that simplify legal decisions without requiring lawyers.

Slide 6: Market Size
- Primary market: content creators, YouTubers, podcasters, indie filmmakers, educators.
- Creator economy value: hundreds of billions globally.
- Adjacent market: digital audio licensing and safe music discovery.

Slide 7: Product
- Discovery page with search, filters, and mixed source results.
- License legend and simplified license labels for beginners.
- Playlist management with save/share/export features.
- Upload/copyright checker page for fingerprint analysis and match reporting.

Slide 8: Team
- Two founders: you and your friend.
- Full-stack product execution with Node.js, Express, HTML/CSS/JS.
- Strong focus on creator workflows and fast, easy music verification.

Slide 9: Business Model
- Free tier for discovery, playlists, and basic verification.
- Paid upgraded tier for legal assumption features and premium verification.
- Additional revenue through Google ads and future licensing partnerships.

Slide 10: Competition
- Existing competitors each offer only one component: discovery or verification.
- Harmoniq combines both, plus playlist management and beginner-friendly licensing.
- Differentiator: an integrated creator workflow, not separate tools.

Slide 11: Financials
- Low initial costs: simple stack, Render deployment, local storage for playlists.
- Revenue plan: ads + paid upgrade tier with legal assumptions.
- Early focus: demo presentation and validating creator demand.
